⚡ Quick Answer / Key Takeaway:

To compress a PDF without losing quality, downsample high-resolution images to 150–200 DPI, remove embedded duplicate fonts, and strip unused metadata. You can automate this process in seconds using PDF.SaveFast's online PDF compressor.

🔍 1. Why Are PDF Files So Large?

Understanding what makes a PDF heavy allows you to optimize it without destroying visual quality:

Uncompressed Images (300+ DPI)
High-resolution digital photos and scanned pages take up over 80% of a typical PDF's file size.
Full Embedded Fonts
Exporting documents often embeds entire font packages rather than subsetting only the characters used.
Hidden Metadata & Revision Logs
Thumbnails, editing histories, and hidden annotations add unnecessary kilobytes.
