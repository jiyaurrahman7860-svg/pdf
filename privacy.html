🛡️ Privacy Policy 🔒 Zero File Storage Policy 📅 Effective: August 9, 2026

Your Privacy & File Security is Our #1 Priority

At PDF.SaveFast, we believe your personal documents and images belong exclusively to you. Learn how our browser-side engineering protects your privacy.

🔒 Core Security Guarantee:

PDF.SaveFast does not view, store, mine, or train models on any uploaded PDF or image files. Processing happens locally in your browser wherever technically possible, ensuring 100% data confidentiality.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike traditional online converters that upload your entire document to external cloud servers, PDF.SaveFast uses modern client-side WebAssembly technology:

  • No Document Content Mining: We do not inspect, extract, or index the text or images inside your files.
  • No User Account Registration: We do not require usernames, passwords, or personal identity verification to access any of our 50+ free tools.

2. Temporary Server File Processing

For complex server-assisted conversions (such as high-fidelity Word to PDF rendering):

  • All data transfers are protected using 256-bit SSL (HTTPS) encryption.
  • Uploaded files are stored temporarily in isolated memory instances.
  • All processed files are automatically and permanently deleted within 60 minutes.

3. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We use standard, lightweight cookies and local storage exclusively to store user interface preferences (such as recent tool history or dark mode settings) and maintain anonymous site analytics to optimize site performance.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

To keep PDF.SaveFast 100% free for everyone worldwide, we partner with reputable ad platforms such as Google AdSense. Third-party vendors may use non-identifiable cookies to serve relevant ads based on prior visits to our website.

5. Contact Privacy Team

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our technical security standards, please contact our Privacy Team at privacy@savefast.in.
